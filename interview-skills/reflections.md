Interview Skills Reflection (STAR)
----
**Situation:**
In the interview skills, I learned about the key stages of preparing for and performing well in job interviews.

**Task:**
My task was to apply the knowledge gained to develop strategies for answering common interview questions and to practice professional body language and confidence.

**Action:**
I studied the STAR method for structuring answers, researched common interview questions, how to prepare for virtual meeting, understood in-person and online interview and learned how to answer interview questions.

**Result:**
By the end of this task, I gained a better understanding of how to prepare for interviews and present myself effectively. I now feel more confident about entering real interviews and can communications my strengths and experiences clearly and professionally.
